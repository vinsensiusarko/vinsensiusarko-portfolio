    Privacy Policy

    Google Play Store Compliant Last Updated: September 10, 2026 Developer: Vinsensius Arka

    Privacy First

    We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data is strictly used to deliver and improve our apps.

    Secure by Design

    All communications are encrypted via industry-standard protocols. Permissions are requested only when required for core features.

    User Control

    You have full control over your data. Request complete deletion of your account and associated records anytime via email.

    1. Introduction & Scope

    Welcome to the Privacy Policy of Vinsensius Arka ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, software, tools, and digital services published and maintained by Vinsensius Arka on the Google Play Store (including but not limited to Mozaic POS App and subsequent releases), as well as our official developer portfolio website (https://vinsensiusarko.web.app / https://vinsensiusarko.github.io).

    We recognize that your privacy is of utmost importance. This document explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is handled, and what choices and rights you have concerning your data. By downloading, installing, or using any of our applications or visiting our website, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

    2. Information We Collect

    Depending on the specific application or feature you use, we may collect the following types of information:

    A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

    • Account Information: If an application requires registration or login, we may collect your name, email address, username, phone number, and organization or store details (for business applications such as point-of-sale systems).
    • Transaction & Inventory Data: For productivity and commercial management apps (such as POS apps), transaction logs, sales entries, product inventory, and customer receipts entered by you are stored securely to support the app's operational functionality.
    • Support & Communications: If you contact us for technical assistance, bug reports, or customer service, we collect your email address, message contents, and any attachments you provide.

    B. Information Collected Automatically (Device & Diagnostic Data)

    When you use our mobile apps or access our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by our systems or third-party SDKs to ensure stability and diagnose errors:

    • Device Information: Device model, hardware specifications, manufacturer, operating system version, system language, and unique device identifiers (e.g., Android ID or advertising ID).
    • Usage & Performance Data: Application launch times, screen views, feature interactions, network status, session durations, and performance benchmarks.
    • Crash Logs & Diagnostics: Real-time stack traces, memory state at time of crash, and error reports generated via Google Play Console and Firebase Crashlytics.

    C. Application Device Permissions

    Our applications may request specific system permissions to deliver essential features. Each permission is requested with explicit disclosure at runtime:

    • Camera: Requested strictly when you choose to scan product barcodes, QR codes, or capture item images within the app. The camera is never accessed in the background or without your active trigger.
    • Storage / Files / Media: Required to export sales reports (PDF/Excel), create local database backups, or load product imagery from your gallery.
    • Internet & Network State: Necessary to sync transactions with cloud databases, verify licensing, and download required updates.
    • Notifications: Used to deliver critical operational reminders, system status updates, or transaction confirmations.

    You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Permissions). Please note that disabling certain permissions may affect specific app capabilities.

    3. How We Use Your Information

    We use the information collected strictly for legitimate purposes, including:

    • Operating, providing, and maintaining the core functionalities of our applications.
    • Managing user authentication, sessions, and data synchronization.
    • Diagnosing crashes, resolving bugs, and optimizing app performance and responsiveness.
    • Responding to user inquiries, customer support tickets, and feature requests.
    • Protecting against fraud, security incidents, and unauthorized access.
    • Complying with applicable legal obligations and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

    Notice: We do NOT engage in the sale, rental, or commercial licensing of your personal information to data brokers, advertising networks, or third parties.

    4. Third-Party Service Providers

    Our applications and website utilize select third-party services to assist in infrastructure, error monitoring, and analytics. These third parties may collect and process information in accordance with their independent privacy policies:

    Provider Purpose Privacy Policy Link
    Google Play Services Core Android system features, licensing, and security APIs Google Privacy Policy
    Firebase Analytics App performance metrics and aggregated usage statistics Firebase Analytics Policy
    Firebase Crashlytics Real-time crash reporting and stability diagnostics Crashlytics Terms
    Google AdMob (if enabled) Displaying relevant non-intrusive advertisements Google Ads Privacy

    5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

    Our portfolio website may use essential cookies and web storage (localStorage / sessionStorage) to preserve user preferences and ensure smooth navigation. We do not use intrusive tracking cookies across external websites. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although some site elements may not function optimally.

    6. Data Retention Policy

    We retain your personal data and application records only for as long as is necessary to fulfill the purposes detailed in this policy, maintain active accounts, or comply with statutory, tax, or legal requirements. When personal data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted, anonymized, or isolated from further processing.

    7. User Rights & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

    In compliance with global data protection laws (including GDPR and CCPA) and Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, you have the following rights:

    • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
    • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of any inaccurate or outdated information.
    • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.
    • Right to Restrict Processing: You may object to or request limitations on how your data is processed.

    How to Request Account & Data Deletion:
    If you wish to delete your account or any personal data associated with our applications, please send an email to:
    vinsensiusarka@gmail.com
    Please use the subject line: Data Deletion Request - [Application Name] and specify your registered email address or username. We will process your request and permanently delete your data within 30 days, sending you an email confirmation upon completion.

    8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

    Our applications and services are not intended for children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without verified parental consent, we will promptly take steps to delete such data from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

    9. Security of Your Information

    We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure. All web and API communications are transmitted using secure HTTPS/TLS encryption. While we strive to protect your data using commercially reasonable means, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

    10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our applications, changes in regulatory guidelines, or updates to Google Play Developer policies. When changes are made, the revised version will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
