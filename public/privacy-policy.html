1 Introduction & Scope

Welcome to the Privacy Policy of Vinsensius Arka ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications, software, tools, and digital services published and maintained under our developer account on the Google Play Store (including but not limited to Mozaic POS App and subsequent releases), as well as our official developer portfolio website.

We respect your personal privacy and are committed to safeguarding user data through transparency and security. By downloading, installing, or accessing any of our mobile applications, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum necessary information required to operate, maintain, and provide the services you request:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account & Profile Details: If an application includes account registration or authentication, we may collect your name, email address, username, phone number, and business/store name (for commercial applications such as point-of-sale systems).
  • Operational & Transaction Data: In business productivity applications, data entered by you (such as sales records, product catalogs, customer receipts, or inventory) is stored securely to enable core app functions.
  • Customer Support Communications: If you contact us for technical assistance, bug reports, or feature requests, we collect your email address, inquiry details, and any screenshots or logs you provide.

B. Information Collected Automatically (Diagnostic Data)

When you run our mobile apps, automated telemetry and performance data may be logged by our systems or verified third-party SDKs to diagnose crashes and optimize reliability:

  • Device Telemetry: Device model, manufacturer, Android OS version, system language, screen resolution, and network state.
  • Crash Logs & Diagnostics: Real-time stack traces, memory state at time of crash, and error reports generated via Google Play Console and Firebase Crashlytics.

3 Application Device Permissions

Our applications request only runtime system permissions that are strictly necessary for specific user-initiated features. Each permission is requested with an in-app explanation:

Camera

Used strictly when you scan product barcodes, QR codes, or take item photos. Never accessed in the background.

Storage / Files / Media

Used to export PDF/Excel sales reports, create local database backups, or import product images from your gallery.

Internet / Network State

Required to synchronize transactions with cloud databases, verify license status, and download updates.

Notifications

Used to deliver operational alerts, transaction confirmations, or system status updates.

You can modify or revoke any permission at any time via your device settings (Settings > Apps > [App Name] > Permissions).

4 How We Use Your Information

We process collected information exclusively for valid, legitimate purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and deliver all core features of our mobile applications.
  • To manage authentication, user accounts, and data synchronization.
  • To identify, debug, and resolve crashes, errors, and performance bottlenecks.
  • To respond to user inquiries, provide customer support, and process feedback.
  • To maintain security, prevent unauthorized usage, and protect user accounts.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and Google Play Developer policies.
Strict Commitment: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data to data brokers, advertising networks, or third parties.

6 Data Retention Policy

We retain personal data and application records only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy, maintain active user accounts, or comply with legal, tax, and accounting standards. When data is no longer needed, it is permanently deleted, anonymized, or securely isolated from further processing.

7 User Rights & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, and global privacy frameworks (such as GDPR and CCPA), you have full control over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of any inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): You may request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

To request permanent deletion of your account and any personal data associated with our applications, please send an email to:

me@vinsensiusarka.id
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request - [Application Name]
  • Details: Provide your registered email address or account username.
  • Timeline: Your request will be fulfilled within 30 days, and you will receive a confirmation email upon completion.

8 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our applications and services are not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete such data from our servers. If you believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9 Security of Your Information

We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—including SSL/TLS encryption for all data transmissions—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, destruction, loss, or alteration. While we employ commercially reasonable security standards, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

10 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements in our applications or updates to Google Play Developer policies. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
